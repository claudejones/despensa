The celebration banner drops a string of papel picado when the player passes a place's can-do check or unlocks a place.

## When to use

- A passed can-do check.
- A new place unlocked.

## When not to use

- A single correct answer. That is a green row, not a celebration.
- More than once on a screen.

## Anatomy

- Container: `rosa-container`, `radius-xl`.
- String of flags in the town's wall colours, each with a cut-out.
- Spanish line in `rotulo-large`.
- What you can now do, in English, in `title`.

## Placement

At the top of the results sheet. It is the screen's one hero moment.

## Motion

Flags drop and settle with the slow spatial spring, staggered by 60 ms.

## Do and don't

- Do: Say what the player can now do, not how many points they earned.
- Don't: Use rosa for anything that is not a celebration.

## Accessibility

- Under reduced motion the flags appear in place with no drop.
- The message is text, not part of the artwork.

## What you provide

The Spanish cheer and the can-do sentence.

## Source

Pueblo original. Uses Material 3 colour roles, the corner radius scale and the Expressive springs. The flags follow the papel picado tradition in general form only.
