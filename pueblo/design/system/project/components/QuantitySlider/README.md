The quantity slider sets how much of something you want, with each stop named in Spanish.

## When to use

- Weights and amounts at the market.
- Any small ordered scale: how spicy, how many, what time.

## When not to use

- Exact large numbers. Use the number pad.
- Settings with two values. Use a switch.

## Anatomy

- Value: the Spanish for the current stop, in `word` style, above the track.
- Track: 16 high; filled part `cobalto`, rest `cobalto-container`, with a gap either side of the handle.
- Handle: a 4 wide, 44 high bar that narrows while pressed.
- Stop marks beneath.

## Placement

In the challenge area, under the request from the list.

## States

- Rest.
- Pressed: the handle narrows.
- Focus: `ink` ring on the handle.

## Motion

The handle follows the finger directly and snaps to a stop with the fast spatial spring.

## Do and don't

- Do: Show the Spanish for the stop, not the number, as the value.
- Do: Use five stops or fewer.
- Don't: Label the stops in English; that removes the Spanish from the task.

## Accessibility

- It is a real range input, so arrow keys and switch access work.
- The handle's touch area is 44 high across the full track.
- The value is announced as it changes.

## What you provide

The stops, each with its Spanish name, and the starting stop.

## Source

Material 3 slider, size XS with stops: 16 track with 8 corners, 4 wide by 44 high handle narrowing to 2 when pressed, 6 gap beside the handle. Read 2026-11-21. Departure: the value is shown as a Spanish phrase above the track instead of in Material's value bubble.
