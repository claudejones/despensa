The results summary closes a challenge: what you got right, what slipped, and what you earned.

## When to use

- The end of every challenge.

## When not to use

- Mid-challenge feedback.

## Anatomy

- Three figures: right answers, hints used, pesos earned.
- A row per word that changed state: its mastery token, the Spanish, the English and what happened.
- One primary button.

## Placement

In a bottom sheet over the challenge, or as its own screen after a can-do check.

## Motion

Tokens morph to their new state one after another with the default spatial spring.

## Do and don't

- Do: List only words whose state changed.
- Do: Say where a missed word goes next.
- Don't: Show a grade or percentage as the headline.
- Don't: Use green and red here; the answers were already marked.

## Accessibility

- Figures have text labels.
- Each row reads as one sentence.

## What you provide

The three figures and the changed words.

## Source

Pueblo original. Built from the card, mastery token and button.
