A place card is one place shown as a row in a list, with what is waiting for you there.

## When to use

- Today's errands.
- Progress by place.

## When not to use

- The map. Use the map marker.

## Anatomy

- Swatch: 56 square in the wall colour with white trim and the place icon.
- Name in `rotulo-small`.
- One line of status in `label`.
- Chevron.

## Placement

In a vertical list with a 12 gap.

## States

- Rest.
- Pressed: the whole card is the target.

## Do and don't

- Do: Say what is waiting in plain words, such as "4 words are slipping".
- Don't: Show a percentage as the only status.

## Accessibility

- The whole card is one target, 80 high.
- On dark wall colours the icon is white.

## What you provide

The place and its status line.

## Source

Pueblo original. Uses Material 3 colour roles, the corner radius scale and the Expressive springs. Follows Material 3 list and card guidance.
