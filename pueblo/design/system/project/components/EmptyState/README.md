An empty state explains why a screen has nothing on it and offers the next step.

## When to use

- No errands due, no achievements yet, an empty phrasebook section.

## When not to use

- Errors. Say what went wrong and how to fix it.
- Loading.

## Anatomy

- A neutral shape with an icon.
- A title in `headline`.
- One or two sentences in `body`.
- One action.

## Placement

Centred in the empty area.

## Do and don't

- Do: Say what to do next.
- Don't: Use a sad or apologetic tone. Nothing has gone wrong.

## Accessibility

- The shape is decorative.
- The action is a real button.

## What you provide

The icon, title, text and action.

## Source

Pueblo original. Uses Material 3 type and colour roles.
