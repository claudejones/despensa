The patience meter shows how much longer a townsperson will wait for your reply.

## When to use

- Timed challenges and the timed rung of a conversation.

## When not to use

- Untimed practice. Leave it off so beginners are not rushed.
- A countdown in seconds. Patience is a mood, not a clock.

## Anatomy

- Pill: `card`, 32 high.
- Timer icon and the word "Patience".
- Five pips in `ink`; spent pips turn `outline-soft` and shrink.

## Placement

Top left of the scene window.

## States

- Five to one pips.
- Empty: the character speaks and the turn ends.

## Motion

A pip shrinks with the fast spatial spring. No flashing.

## Do and don't

- Do: Change the character's expression at three pips and at one.
- Don't: Colour it red. Red means a wrong answer and nothing else.

## Accessibility

- The pips carry a text alternative, such as "Patience 2 of 5".
- Offer a setting that turns timing off.

## What you provide

The number of pips left.

## Source

Pueblo original. Uses Material 3 colour roles, the corner radius scale and the Expressive springs.
