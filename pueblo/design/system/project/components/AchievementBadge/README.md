An achievement badge marks something the player can now do in Spanish.

## When to use

- Can-do checks passed and skills shown in play.

## When not to use

- Counting taps, days or points. Volume is not an achievement.

## Anatomy

- Emblem: burst shape in `rosa`, 64 across, with an icon.
- Name: what you did, in `title`.
- How: one line in `label`.

## Placement

On the Achievements screen and in results when first earned.

## States

- Earned.
- Not yet earned: neutral circle, and the way to earn it.

## Motion

Earning morphs circle to burst with the slow spatial spring.

## Do and don't

- Do: Name the ability in the past tense, as something done.
- Don't: Hide how a badge is earned.

## Accessibility

- Earned and unearned differ by shape, icon and tone.

## What you provide

The name, the how line, an icon and whether it is earned.

## Source

Pueblo original. Uses Material 3 colour roles, the corner radius scale and the Expressive springs.
