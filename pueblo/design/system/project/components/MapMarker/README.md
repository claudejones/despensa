A map marker is one place on the town map, showing whether it is locked, open or mastered.

## When to use

- The town map.

## When not to use

- Lists. Use the place card there.

## Anatomy

- Emblem: 72 across, in the place's wall colour, with the place icon.
- Name in the rótulo face.
- State line in `label-small`.

## Placement

On the map at the place's building.

## States

- Locked: neutral circle with a lock.
- Open: squircle in the wall colour, with progress.
- Mastered: sunny shape in the wall colour.

## Motion

Unlocking morphs circle to squircle; mastering morphs squircle to sunny. This is the map's hero moment.

## Do and don't

- Do: Use the wall colour here; the map is art, not a control surface.
- Don't: Rely on the shape alone. The state line is always shown.

## Accessibility

- Markers are 96 wide and at least 48 high to tap.
- State is given in words.

## What you provide

The place, its state and its progress.

## Source

Pueblo original. Uses Material 3 colour roles, the corner radius scale and the Expressive springs.
