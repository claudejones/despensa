The top bar names where you are and holds the way back.

## When to use

- Every screen except the title screen.

## When not to use

- Actions for the challenge. Those sit at the bottom, in thumb reach.

## Anatomy

- Back icon button on the left, inside a place.
- Title: the place name in `rotulo-small`, or a plain screen name in `headline`.
- One trailing item: the coin chip in a place, Settings elsewhere.

## Placement

Top of the screen, full width, on `cal`.

## States

- Rest.
- The title truncates with an ellipsis; it never wraps.

## Do and don't

- Do: Use the rótulo face for place names only.
- Don't: Put more than one trailing item in the bar.

## Accessibility

- The back button is labelled with where it goes, such as "Back to town".
- The title is the page heading.

## What you provide

The title, whether it is a place, and the trailing item.

## Source

Material 3 app bar, small: 64 high, 4 side padding, 24 icons, one action. Read 2026-11-21. Departure: the title uses the rótulo face at 24/32 where Material uses title-large.
