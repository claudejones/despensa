The colour code

Five colours, five jobs. Each job keeps its colour everywhere in the game, and every status also carries an icon and a word.

Act cobaltoTalavera cobalt on white glaze. Buttons, selection.
Correct nopalCactus green. Always with a check.
Wrong cochinillaCochineal red. Nothing else is ever red.
Money ocreIzamal ochre. Coins and prices only.
Celebrate rosaRosa mexicano. A passed check, a new place.

Grounds and text

The interface stays quiet so the town's art carries the colour. Ratios are WCAG contrast of the text on its ground.

Place walls

Every building is painted one flat colour with white trim, as in a Yucatán town. These live in the pixel art and on the map only, never in a button.

Sources for the names: Talavera cobalt, cochineal red, Maya blue, añil indigo, Izamal ochre, rosa mexicano and sea-snail purple are all traditional Mexican colours.
