Five overlays, lightest first

Use the lightest one that does the job. Each is a component with its own page; this board says which to reach for.

Info tip

Explains one thing when its info button is pressed. Two sentences at most.

Snackbar

Something just happened. One line, at most one action such as Undo. It leaves by itself.

Bottom sheet

Hints, results, short choices. Rises over the screen; drag it down or tap outside to close.

Dialog

Cannot be undone. Leaving a check, resetting progress. Two named actions, never OK and Cancel.

Side sheet

Wide screens only. What is a bottom sheet or a whole screen on a phone opens as a panel at the edge.

The same hint, phone and wide

A bottom sheet on a phone becomes a side sheet at 600 wide and up. The content is the same; only where it sits changes.

Rules for all of them

One at a timeNever a sheet over a sheet or a dialog over a sheet. Close the first, then open the next.
Always a way outEvery overlay closes with a visible control, not only by dragging or tapping outside.
Never for right or wrongAn answer's result is shown on the answer itself, never in a pop-up.
