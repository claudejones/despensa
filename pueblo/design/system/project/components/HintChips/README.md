Hint chips break one Spanish line into its words, each with its literal English beneath.

## When to use

- The hint sheet, when the player asks for help with a line.

## When not to use

- Showing by default. A hint is asked for.
- During a can-do check.

## Anatomy

- One chip per word: `cal-low`, `radius-md`.
- Spanish in `word` style above, English in `label` below.

## Placement

In a bottom sheet over the challenge.

## Do and don't

- Do: Give the literal meaning, so the player sees how the Spanish is built.
- Don't: Give the natural English translation here; that is the subtitle's job.

## Accessibility

- Chips wrap; no horizontal scrolling.
- Each chip reads as "Spanish, meaning".

## What you provide

The words and their literal meanings.

## Source

Pueblo original. Uses Material 3 colour roles, the corner radius scale and the Expressive springs.
