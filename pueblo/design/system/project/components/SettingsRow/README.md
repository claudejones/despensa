A settings row holds one setting: its icon, name, a line of explanation and its control.

## When to use

- The Settings screen.

## When not to use

- Game content. Use the list row for prompts.

## Anatomy

- Leading icon in `ink`.
- Name in `title`, explanation in `label`.
- Trailing control: a switch, or a chevron to a further choice.
- 72 high, rows divided by `outline-soft`.

## Placement

Grouped inside a card.

## Do and don't

- Do: Explain what the setting does in one plain sentence.
- Don't: Put two controls in one row.

## Accessibility

- The whole row toggles its switch.
- The control's label is the row's name.

## What you provide

The icon, name, explanation and control.
