A verb is the word in a sentence that shows an action (eat, run, pay), an event (happen, rain), or a state of being (be, have, seem). Every sentence has one — it's the engine. In a Spanish dictionary, verbs end in -ar, -er, or -ir: hablar (to speak), comer (to eat), vivir (to live).
