# NFL Intelligence & Kalshi Portfolio Lab

Development branch foundation. No live picks or trades.

## Product
- Weeks: select season/week, load `data/YYYY/week-NN.json`; missing weeks display next scheduled research date from manifest.
- Games: logo, full team name, W-L-T record, actual date/daypart/time, expandable Form, Injuries, Matchup, Market, Historical insights.
- AI Picks: independent winner prediction, calibrated probability and rationale; freeze before kickoff. Never derive a football prediction from a desired Kalshi payout.
- Combos: research-based candidate plans A–E; price and validate before recommendation.
- Kalshi: import AI candidate or build personal ticket, edit stake/YES-NO/quote/fees, monitor and close; local storage plus export/import.
- Dashboard: actual realized P&L, ROI, open risk, AI calibration, strategy performance and decision log.

## Principles
All team mentions show full name and record. Label Single Game Bet or exact Combo type. Group legs by weekday/date and Morning/Afternoon/Evening, never generic Next Day. Collapsed accordion has summary; expanded shows sourced bullets. Use consistent down/up chevrons. Icons accompany text. Team logos must use licensed/approved assets and abbreviation fallback.
