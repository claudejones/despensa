# Portfolio strategy contract

Football research first: records, opponent quality, recent form, QB and injuries, travel, rest, efficiency, market disagreement. Do not pick weak-record underdogs solely for payout. Predictions independent from Kalshi. Keep estimated probability and Kalshi market price separate.

A: researched early single + combo with later higher-probability team; check conditional capital recovery.
B: simultaneous-game combos; consider live repricing without claiming a guaranteed cash-out.
C: staggered games across actual kickoff times and days, with explicit exit checkpoints.
D: undefeated streak continuation, subject to opponent strength.
E: winless/one-win team NO selections, subject to opponent strength and contract equivalence.

Do not force a strategy or number of tickets. Compare singles, combos, opposing paths and hybrids using expected net value, maximum loss, shared-game exposure, cost and time. A hedge is only beneficial if its cost is justified. Cash-out prices must be executable, fees included; never infer a guaranteed future buyer. A $6 Buffalo historical example returned $7.37 single + $7.32 executed combo sale = $8.69 realized net (144.8% ROI), a regression case, not a profit guarantee.

Presentation: full team name and W-L-T record whenever team shown; YES/NO explicit; label ticket type, kickoff daypart and exact date/time, stake, Kalshi %, gross payout, net and exit plan. Do not mix exploratory tickets with recommendations. Never place trades.
